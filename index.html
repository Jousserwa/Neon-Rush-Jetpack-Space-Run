// Controls

How to Play

01

Launch Your Ship

Tap "START RUSH" from the main screen. Your ship launches automatically into the neon corridor — get ready fast.

02

Swipe to Dodge

Swipe left or right to change lanes. Swipe up to jump, swipe down to slide under barriers. Every millisecond counts.

03

Collect Gems

Fly through glowing gems scattered across the track. Gems power up your ship and unlock new skins in the customization deck.

04

Race Ghost Trails

Follow the ghost telemetry paths left by top players. Matching their route gives you score multipliers and pushes your global ranking.

05

Beat Your Best

Each run ends when you crash. Check your score, compete globally, and push further every time. How far can you go?
